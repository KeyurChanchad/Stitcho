Stitcho – Account & Data Deletion

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Stitcho uses Google Sign-In to authenticate users and provide access to the application. Stitcho does not operate a backend server or maintain a database of Stitcho user accounts.

Information associated with your Stitcho sign-in session and your embroidery calculation data are stored locally on your device.

Important: Deleting your Stitcho account/data does not delete your Google Account. Your Google Account is managed independently by Google.

What Happens When You Delete Your Stitcho Account?

When you delete your Stitcho account/data, Stitcho data stored locally on your device is removed. This includes, where applicable:

  • Google Sign-In information stored by Stitcho
  • Local user/session information
  • Embroidery calculation history
  • Saved calculation inputs and results
  • Local application preferences and settings

Stitcho does not maintain a server-side database containing this information.

How to Delete Your Stitcho Data

  1. From the Stitcho app: Use the Delete Account / Delete My Data option, if available. The app will remove Stitcho data stored locally on your device and sign you out.
  2. From Android settings: Clear Stitcho's application data through your device's app settings. This removes locally stored Stitcho application data.
  3. Uninstall Stitcho: Uninstalling the application may remove Stitcho's locally stored application data from your device.

Request Deletion by Email

If you believe Stitcho may have information associated with you that is not stored locally on your device, you can submit a deletion request by contacting us.

Please include the email address associated with your Stitcho Google Sign-In account so that we can identify the relevant request.

No Server-Side Calculation History

Stitcho does not upload or store your embroidery calculation history on our own servers. Calculation inputs, results, and history are stored locally on your device.

Therefore, there is no server-side calculation history maintained in a Stitcho database that needs to be deleted.

Google Sign-In

Stitcho uses Google Sign-In as its authentication provider. Stitcho does not delete or modify your Google Account when you delete your Stitcho data.

Google may independently retain or process information associated with your Google Account according to Google's policies.
