Stitcho – Account & Data Deletion

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

Stitcho is designed as a privacy-first, offline-capable embroidery design calculation tool. Stitcho does not require user accounts, does not use Google Sign-In, and does not operate any backend server or database.

Summary: Because Stitcho does not maintain user accounts or store personal data on any server, there is no remote account or profile to delete. All application data is stored 100% locally on your device.

What Data Is Stored?

All data created while using Stitcho is saved strictly in local storage on your physical device. This includes:

  • Saved embroidery design calculation entries and history
  • Custom section inputs, stitch numbers, and rates
  • Local application state and preferences

None of this information is ever uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on external servers.

How to Delete Your Data

Since all data exists solely on your device, you have complete control over deleting it at any time:

  1. From inside the Stitcho app: Open the History modal and delete individual records using the delete action, or clear current form values with the "Clear All" button.
  2. Via Device Settings: Go to your device's Settings > Apps > Stitcho > Storage, and tap Clear Data or Clear Storage. This completely wipes all locally stored history and preferences.
  3. Uninstall Stitcho: Uninstalling the application immediately removes the application and all associated local data from your device.

No Third-Party Advertising or Tracking

Stitcho does not integrate Google AdMob or any third-party advertising SDKs. No personal information or identifiers are collected or shared for advertising purposes.
