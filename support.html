Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is Stitcho?

Stitcho is an offline embroidery design calculation utility built for saree makers, embroidery designers, and textile manufacturers. It allows quick calculation of total stitch counts, rates per thousand stitches, section costs, and total bill amounts.

2. Does Stitcho require an internet connection or account?

No. Stitcho operates completely offline and does not require account creation, login, or internet connectivity. All calculations are performed on your device.

3. How do I export or share my calculations?

You can tap the Share on WhatsApp / PDF button on any calculation. Stitcho automatically generates a formatted PDF document containing a breakdown of the design name, sections, stitches, rates, and total cost ready to share.

4. How do I save and manage calculation history?

Enter a Design No / Name and tap Save Design. You can view all saved calculations anytime by tapping the History button, where you can reload or delete existing records.

5. How can I delete my saved data?

You can delete individual records from the History screen, clear current inputs with the Clear button, or remove all data via device settings or app uninstallation.
