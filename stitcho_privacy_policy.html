Privacy Policy for Stitcho

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

Stitcho ("we", "our", "us", or the "App") is an embroidery design calculation application that helps users perform embroidery-related calculations and maintain calculation history.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information Stitcho handles, how it is used, and how it is stored.

1. Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is required to use Stitcho. When you sign in with Google, the App may receive basic information associated with your Google account, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Google account/profile identifier
  • Profile information made available by Google through the sign-in process

Stitcho does not operate its own backend server or user database for storing your Google account information. The information required by the App for the signed-in session is stored locally on your device.

When you sign out of Stitcho, the locally stored Google Sign-In session information is deleted from the App's local storage. Google may retain information independently according to Google's own policies.

2. Calculation Data and History

Stitcho may store information entered or generated while using its calculation features, including:

  • Embroidery calculation inputs
  • Calculation results
  • Calculation history
  • App preferences and settings

This information is stored locally on your device. Stitcho does not upload or store your calculation history on our own backend server.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by Stitcho is used to:

  • Provide and operate the application's features
  • Perform embroidery-related calculations
  • Display your calculation history
  • Maintain local app preferences and settings
  • Provide the required Google Sign-In functionality
  • Display advertisements

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Local Storage

Stitcho is designed to store its application data locally on your device. Your calculation inputs, results, history, and applicable app preferences are not maintained on our own backend servers.

Because this information is stored locally, clearing the application's data, resetting the application, or uninstalling Stitcho may permanently delete locally stored information.

5. Advertising - Google AdMob

Stitcho uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. During development and testing, the App may use Google's test/debug advertising configuration or test ad identifiers. Production releases may use Google's production advertising configuration.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or use certain information for advertising and measurement purposes. Depending on the user's device, region, consent choices, and the advertising configuration, this may include information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and app information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address or other signals
  • Ad interaction and performance information

Google may use this information to provide, measure, and personalize advertisements where permitted by applicable law and user consent.

Google's processing of information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policy and terms.

6. Third-Party Services

Stitcho uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In - required for authentication and access to the App.
  • Google AdMob - used to display advertisements.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Stitcho does not control the privacy practices of these third-party service providers.

7. Data Sharing

Stitcho does not sell or rent your personal information.

We do not share your locally stored calculation history with third parties because calculation history is stored locally on your device and is not maintained on our backend servers.

However, information may be processed by third-party services integrated into the App, including Google Sign-In and Google AdMob, as necessary for those services to function.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by Stitcho. Since most Stitcho application data is stored locally on your device, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device, operating system, and device access controls.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Deletion

Stitcho does not maintain a backend database containing your calculation history.

You can remove locally stored Stitcho data by:

  • Signing out of Stitcho, which removes locally stored Google Sign-In session information.
  • Clearing the application's data through your device settings.
  • Uninstalling Stitcho from your device.

Data processed independently by third-party services, such as Google, may be subject to those services' own retention and deletion policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Stitcho is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable minimum age for providing consent to data processing. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our own backend because Stitcho does not operate a backend database for user data.

11. International Data Processing

Third-party services integrated into Stitcho, including Google services, may process information on servers located in countries other than your country of residence. Such processing is subject to the providers' privacy policies, terms, and applicable laws.

12. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device and location, you may have choices regarding:

  • Whether to sign in to Stitcho using Google
  • Advertising and advertising personalization settings
  • Device permissions
  • Removing locally stored application data
  • Uninstalling the application

You may also manage certain Google privacy and advertising settings through your Google account and device settings.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Stitcho, third-party services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, we will update the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
